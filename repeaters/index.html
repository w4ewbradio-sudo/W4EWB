What the buttons do
Listen, Low latency, Denoise, Chime
Listen
Plays whichever repeater the scanner has stopped on. Between transmissions you'll hear silence while it keeps scanning.
Low latency
Normally the audio comes as a compressed stream that your browser buffers, so you hear things a few seconds after they happen. Low latency sends raw audio and plays it right away, about half a second behind the air. It uses about four times the data (roughly 60 MB an hour instead of 15) and stops if your phone locks or you switch apps, so leave it off for pocket listening. It may take a few seconds to start.
Denoise
Runs the audio through RNNoise, an AI noise filter on the station's Raspberry Pi. It takes out hiss and static, which helps most on weak, noisy signals. Voices can sound a little processed, and a very weak signal can drop out. Works with or without Low latency.
Chime
Plays a short ping when a repeater comes on the air, so you can leave the page open without listening. Your browser needs one tap on the page before it will make a sound.
