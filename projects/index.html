Biometric
Point a webcam at your face and it measures your heart rate from the colour of your skin, reads your expression, and watches for signs of tiredness. When it cannot read something it says so. It runs in your browser and nothing leaves your device.

A music workstation for Strudel, the live-coding language. Pick a genre, stack up layers, turn the knobs, and it writes the code and plays it as you go.

Penny Stock Oracle
A paper-trading experiment. An AI picked penny stocks every trading day from March 31 to April 30, 2026, with pretend money. It is paused now, and the picks and research log are archived. Not financial advice.
